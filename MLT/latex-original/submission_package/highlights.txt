Highlights
- Acoustic similarity of stimuli quantified as distance between 16-D feature vectors
- Descriptors: spectral centroid, F0, peak-to-average ratio and 13 MFCCs
- Closest stimulus pair (d = 0.736) was correctly identified in only 20.5% of trials
- Distance and response accuracy were not monotonically related across pairs
- Distance matrices can flag overly similar stimuli before auditory testing
